Here's everything you need to know about getting to the wedding and getting home safely.

Shuttle Service

  • Shuttles will depart from Beacon Train Station directly to Blooming Hill Farm at a specific time prior to the ceremony. If you're arriving by train, please plan to arrive within the designated window so you don't miss the magic. (Exact times coming soon!)

  • At the end of the reception, shuttles will carry guests from the farm to either the afterparty or the Hampton Inn Newburgh. You'll be in good hands either way.

  • Additional shuttles will run from the afterparty back to the hotel throughout the night and at its close, so you can stay as long as your heart desires.

Arriving by Train from NYC

Metro-North from Grand Central Terminal to Beacon Train Station (approx. 1.5 hours). Beacon is about 30 minutes from the venue.

Local Car Services

Heads up: Uber and Lyft wait times in this area can run 20–30 minutes, so we recommend pre-scheduling a car service if you're arranging your own ride.

  • Monroe Car Service: (845) 964-8888

  • Goshen Taxi: (845) 615-9299

  • Geo Taxi Service: (845) 213-5639

  • Speedy Car Service: (845) 783-6651

  • Village Taxi: (845) 783-6112

Parking

Parking is available on-site at Blooming Hill Farm for guests who are driving.
