Can I bring a date/plus-one?

At this time we are not accepting plus-ones. There might be some exceptions to this as spots on the guestlist may open up as RSVPs trickle in.

Are children welcome?

No, this is an adults-only celebration.

What should I wear?

Please see the Dress Code section for details!

What's the weather like in August?

Typically 70–85°F (21–30°C) with a possibility of rain.

Is the ceremony outdoors?

Yes, with a backup tent and umbrellas available in case of rain.

Is the venue wheelchair accessible?

Yes, a golf cart is available and we will set up chairs with backs in our front two rows.

When should I RSVP by?

Early July 2026 (6 weeks before the wedding).

Can I take photos?

We will be handing out several cameras to the guests to help us document, but otherwise please refrain from taking photos during the ceremony. We would also prefer that phone use be limited throughout the event and that phones be put away when not in use.

Where should I park?

Blooming Hill Farm has parking available.

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive between 3:45–4:00 pm.

How late does the party go?

The reception at Blooming Hill Farm concludes at 10:00 pm. An afterparty will follow — details can be found here.
